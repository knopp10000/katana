## Ode to Katana

Soft ears perked up, she trots into the room,  
A Shiba Inu chasing away the gloom.  
Katana the dog, with her fox-like face,  
Brings warmth and wonder to every meeting place.  

She curls at our feet as the agendas fly,  
A reminder to pause, to breathe, to sigh.  
Her amber eyes hold secrets of the wild,  
Yet here she sits, gentle as a child.  

No standup is dull when Katana is near,  
A tail-wagging signal that joy is here.  
She owns every room with a quiet grace,  
Our favorite colleague — the fluffiest face.
